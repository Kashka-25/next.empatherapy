About the film: a 13-second film, just if you like. A drop of honey gathers on a wooden honey dipper while the words “Take a gentle breath in…” appear. The drop falls, and as “And slowly let it go…” appears, the honey flows out and writes the name Empatherapy in joined, flowing golden script. Below, the words “Breathe along if you like, or simply watch the honey.” Then the honey script drips away, the scene turns to warm parchment, and honey drops fall into a teacup of chamomile tea with lemon and a chamomile flower; the cup fills, honey runs over the rim and steam rises. The name Empatherapy rests above the cup, with “Discover new pathways to yourself.” below it.
